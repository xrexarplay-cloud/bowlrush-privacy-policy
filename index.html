BowlRush – Privacy Policy

Developed by ExarPlay.
Effective Date: 30 September 2025

1. Introduction

ExarPlay is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how BowlRush handles your information while you play our single-player bowling VR game on Meta Quest devices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

BowlRush does not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Sensitive information such as financial, biometric, or health information
  • Advertising data, third-party tracking data, or analytics data

3. Information Stored Locally

BowlRush may store limited gameplay-related information locally on your Meta Quest device, including:

```
  • Gameplay progress
  • Scores and achievements
  • Player preferences, such as control and audio settings

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to ExarPlay or any third party. Locally stored game data may be removed when you uninstall the application or clear its application data.

```

4. Data Security

BowlRush does not transmit personal information to external servers. Gameplay-related information that is stored locally remains on the user's device.

5. Children’s Privacy

BowlRush is intended for players aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you have questions or concerns regarding privacy, please contact us using the information provided below.

6. Third-Party Services

BowlRush does not use third-party services for collecting or processing personal information.

7. Your Rights

Because BowlRush does not collect or maintain personal information on external servers, there is generally no personal information held by ExarPlay to access, correct, or delete.

```

You can remove locally stored game data by uninstalling BowlRush or clearing its application data where supported by your device.

For privacy-related questions, you may contact ExarPlay using the contact information below.

```

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
